# Sepia fact sheet

> Research prototype, not for clinical use. Generated by `python -m eval.fact_sheet` from the data root and `artifacts/eval/*.json`; edit the generator, not this file.

## Data units

- **400 BACH microscopy images** (100 Benign, 100 InSitu, 100 Invasive, 100 Normal), each 2048 x 1536 px at 0.42 µm/px. These are single microscopy fields, **not whole-slide images**.
- Each image is read as a 3 x 4 grid of 512-px regions: 12 regions per image, 4,800 regions in total. The production model reads each region once (no rotation/flip averaging); CONCH and MUSK features average 8 rotations/flips per region.
- BACH also ships 30 whole-slide images (10 annotated); Sepia uses them only for the simulated margin windows and concept checks.
- Patient IDs are known for 145 images (29 patients); images without a patient ID are grouped in blocks of 5 consecutive file numbers. Intervals resample these groups.

## Model selection vs testing

- Cross-validation: 5-fold x 3 repeats, patient-grouped, inside the 218 training images only; used to choose the model, never to report test performance. Metric log_loss; rule: one_se: simplest (settings risk_model.complexity, then smaller C) within 1 SE of the best.
- Eligible within one standard error: uni2h_lr@C=0.001, uni2h512_lr@C=0.001, hoptimus0_512_lr@C=0.001, abmil, fusion_top3. Chosen: `uni2h512_lr`.
- Testing: one held-out THUNDER test split of 132 images, scored once for reporting.

## Calibration and confidence sets

- Temperature 0.342, fit on train_oof; ECE 0.184 before, 0.055 after (10 equal-mass bins).
- Conformal threshold fit on 50 calibration images; alpha 0.1. Tier set `aps_marginal_nonempty`: coverage 91.7%, mean size 1.30. The tier set (APS, never empty) was chosen after viewing test coverage; pre-registered LAC covered 72.0%. Disclosed in docs/RESULTS.md.
- Displayed percentages are temperature-scaled class probabilities for this image. They are not probabilities of progression, recurrence or survival.

## Reference distributions behind displayed scores

- **concept scores:** z = (score - mean) / SD over clean, QC-passing tiles of the 218 training images; 'above normal' means above the training-image average for that feature, not a population norm
- **ood percentile:** mean cosine distance of a read's tiles to their k = 10 nearest TTA-averaged training tiles (CONCH), expressed as a percentile of the 50 clean calibration images
- **similar cases:** k = 5 nearest training images by cosine similarity of the mean CONCH embedding; the library is the 218 training images only, so a test image never retrieves itself or another test image
- **look alike:** the nearest training image whose label differs from the predicted class
- **temperature:** fit on out-of-fold logits of the training images
- **conformal threshold:** fit on the 50 calibration images
- **margin threshold:** from 30 benign calibration images (simulated margin read)

## Who did what: pretrained, trained by the team, rules, language models

- Pretrained, frozen: **UNI2-h (MahmoodLab/UNI2-h)**: production image encoder
- Pretrained, frozen: **CONCH (MahmoodLab/CONCH)**: text-aligned concept scores, similar-case retrieval, out-of-distribution distance

- Pretrained, frozen: **H-optimus-0, Virchow2, Phikon-v2, PathGen-CLIP**: evaluated as alternatives; not in production

- Trained by the team: temperature scaling and APS conformal threshold
- Trained by the team: concept vectors from caption-mined Quilt/PathGen images; concept-bottleneck comparison models
- Trained by the team: attention-based MIL (ABMIL) and LoRA fine-tunes of UNI2-h (evaluated, not production)
- Trained by the team: reliability combiner, CONCH tile probe for the risk heatmap, simulated-margin probe
- Rule-based: worklist tiers and the safety rule
- Rule-based: drafted follow-up orders (never placed)

## Three different time numbers

1. **Queue simulation** (simulated reading day, not a measured workflow): Poisson, 6 per hour for 8 h; cases drawn uniformly with replacement from the clean test split; reader: one reader, fixed 9 min per case, non-preemptive, works overtime until empty. Results in `artifacts/eval/queue_sim.json`.
2. **Model latency** (measured): pending the TCGA production run.
3. **Clinical turnaround**: see config/literature.yaml (cited benchmarks); not measured by Sepia.
